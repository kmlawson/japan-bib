Note: The database is made up mostly of books, pamphlets, and standalone reports etc. as they are easier to locate and link to.

Words are ANDed; diacritics and case are ignored (soseki finds Sōseki). Use quotes for phrases, the prefixes author:, title:, source: (the bibliography an entry comes from, e.g. source:borton) and other:, and -word to exclude. With something typed in the search box the best matches come first - the whole phrase in the title, then the words in the title and how close together they are, then the author. Click a column to sort by it instead, or a row for full details. By default only books with a copy online are listed - put type:article, type:chapter, type:periodical or type:all in the search box for the rest, and set Show to All for entries with no copy.

In addition to drawing on database searches and my own Zotero library, many of the sources were found in:

  • Fukuda, Naomi, ed. Union Catalog of Books on Japan in Western Languages. International House Library, 1968.
  • Borton, Hugh, Serge Elisséeff, William W. Lockwood and John C. Pelzel, comps. A Selected List of Books and Articles on Japan in English, French and German. Revised and enlarged. Cambridge, Mass.: Published by the Harvard University Press for the Harvard-Yenching Institute, 1954.
  • Dower, John W., with Timothy S. George. Japanese History and Culture from Ancient to Modern Times: Seven Basic Bibliographies. Second edition, revised and updated. Princeton: Markus Wiener Publishers, 1995.
  • Asiatic Society of Japan. Catalogue of the Books and Manuscripts in the Library of the Asiatic Society of Japan. Tōkyō, 1888. (Its Japan-related books, periodicals excepted.)
  • International Research Center for Japanese Studies (Nichibunken). 日本関係欧文図書目録 (Catalogue of Western-language books on Japan). Kyoto: Nichibunken.
  • Nachod, Oskar. Bibliography of the Japanese Empire 1906–1926. London: Goldston, 1928. (Focusing on its book entries)
  • Wenckstern, Friedrich von. A Bibliography of the Japanese Empire, vol. II: 1894–1906, with a supplement to Pagès and Palmgren's list of the Swedish literature. Tokyo: Maruzen, 1907. (Focusing on its book entries)
  • Henshall, Kenneth. Historical Dictionary of Japan to 1945. Lanham, Toronto and Plymouth, UK: The Scarecrow Press, Inc., 2014.

If you are located in the United States, many of the works listed here that do not have a link might be viewable through HathiTrust. Only the HathiTrust links which are viewable outside the United States have been included here. Many links to these books can be found on the extensive Online Books Page edited by John Mark Ockerbloom.
